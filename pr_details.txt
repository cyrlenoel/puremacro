Title: ⚡ Bolt: Vectorize document upserts in _upsert_rows
Body:
💡 What: Replaced the N+1 SELECT and INSERT/UPDATE queries in _upsert_rows with batch queries using itertools.islice, pulling 900 records at a time, followed by cur.executemany statements.
🎯 Why: Iterating with an explicit cur.execute in a for loop led to unnecessary overhead and N+1 query problems. By checking existence via an IN clause and doing batch inserts/updates with executemany, the overall performance is significantly improved.
📊 Impact: Microbenchmarks for an insert/unchanged/updated payload of 10,000 documents show that upsert processing time drops from 0.27s to 0.20s for inserts, 0.16s to 0.12s for unchanged logic, and 0.20s to 0.15s for updates.
🔬 Measurement: Using time.time() differences across bulk runs of 10k documents in test_bench.db.
