### 💡 What
Replaced repeated `np.concatenate` allocations inside hot loop iterations with vector slicing, reshaping and in-place assignment (`buf[:, :-1] = buf[:, 1:]`).

### 🎯 Why
In simulation and bootstrap loops, repeatedly using `np.concatenate` across `T` time steps allocates new memory each iteration, resulting in unnecessary garbage collection and bottleneck performance. Shifting arrays in-place or slicing and reshaping avoids these continuous reallocations.

### 📊 Impact
Microbenchmarks demonstrate a meaningful drop in computation time for time-series buffer roll forwards and simulation builds, speeding up operations like `bootstrap_bands` without affecting correctness.
